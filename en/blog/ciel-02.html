Astronomy

Monthly Sky: February 2025

Monthly observation series: constellations, planets, and astronomical events.

February offers a rich sky filled with winter constellations and highly visible planets.

Constellations of the month

Orion (the Hunter)

Date / visibility: All month, all night

Description: Orion is one of the most iconic winter constellations.

Tips: Use Orion’s belt as a quick reference to locate it.

  • Betelgeuse
  • Rigel
  • M42 (Orion Nebula)

Taurus

Date / visibility: February evenings

Description: Easily recognizable thanks to the Pleiades cluster.

Tips: Follow Orion westward to find it.

  • Pleiades
  • Aldebaran

Visible planets

Venus

Date / visibility: Dawn

Description: Extremely bright morning star.

Tips: Visible in the east before sunrise.

Mars

Date / visibility: Late night

Description: Easily identifiable red planet.

Tips: Observe from a dark sky area.

Astronomical events

Moon - Jupiter conjunction

Date: February 6

Description: Apparent close approach between the Moon and Jupiter.

Tips: Observe just after sunset.
